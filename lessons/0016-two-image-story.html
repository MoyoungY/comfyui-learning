Lesson 16 / 10 minutes / Choose your tools

A two-image story

Create a tiny “before and after” story using a deliberate local change.

The brief

ImageVisible content
1 · BeforeA white fox beneath a tree, with an empty ground patch nearby.
2 · AfterThe same view, with small yellow flowers appearing in that patch.

Use pixel-art style for both. Keep the task focused on style and composition; no scarf, exact character identity, or rendered text is required. Captions can be added separately later.

Plan before generating

Choose how to create image 2: regenerate the whole image from text, or edit a masked patch in image 1. Which fits the brief better, and why? Tell the agent your choice for feedback.

Make image 1 after feedback

Use a copy of your working pixel-art workflow or an existing result if it meets the brief. Keep a small patch of ground free of flowers. Save its PNG and workflow. Use the tools you already tested; new models are not required.

Make image 2

Use the plan you selected. Keep the pixel-art style settings consistent. If combining a local edit with ControlNet, first consider whether the old edge map would constrain the region where flowers should appear; discuss the choice before adding extra controls. Review inpainting and LoRA only as needed.

Feedback and finish

Show or describe the pair: are flowers clearly new, is the view still recognizable, and do the styles match? If a step fails, report the specific symptom and change one factor before trying again. Save both PNGs, workflows, and source inputs.

Success is a readable local story change with coherent appearance, not a perfect character-consistency system. Stop once the brief is met.

Ask the agent any follow-up questions while building. Primary refresher: official inpainting guide.
