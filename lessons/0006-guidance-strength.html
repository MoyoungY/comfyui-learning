Lesson 06 / 5 minutes / Controlled comparison

Guidance strength

Choose guidance deliberately for your illustration instead of treating a larger number as better.

Recall

Why keep the seed fixed when comparing two CFG values?

One new control

KSampler’s cfg means classifier-free guidance scale. It controls how strongly the positive-versus-negative conditioning difference influences sampling. Increasing guidance is not a guarantee of a better image. Official parameter guide; original guidance paper.

Predict first

For CFG 3 versus CFG 10, which do you expect to apply stronger guidance? Does that alone tell you which watercolor illustration you will prefer? Tell the agent your prediction before running.

Compare

  1. Keep seed 43 and seed control fixed; use the same SD1.5 checkpoint and existing white-fox prompt. Keep the negative prompt unchanged.
  2. Keep 512 × 512, batch 1, steps 20, euler, normal, and denoise 1.0.
  3. Run with CFG 3 and keep the image.
  4. Change only CFG to 10 and run again.
  5. Compare prompt details, softness of watercolor, contrast, and any unwanted visual effects.

The values 3 and 10 are comparison points for this exercise, not universal recommendations.

Feedback

Report one visible difference and which image better fits your intended illustration. If the difference is small, say so; we are observing this pair rather than forcing a conclusion. If the comparison is confusing, check that the seed, prompts, and other settings stayed fixed.

Checkpoint: does higher guidance necessarily improve the result? Support your answer with your observations. Ask the agent any follow-up questions before moving on.
