Canny preview

Load Image.IMAGE → Canny.image
Canny.IMAGE → Preview Image.images

Built-in Canny defaults: low_threshold 0.4, high_threshold 0.8. White lines mark detected edges; black areas have no detected edges. Output is IMAGE, not MASK.

Inspect for recognizable subject outlines. A successful preview establishes edge extraction, not ControlNet model loading or controlled generation.
